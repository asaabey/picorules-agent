# Picorules AI Infographic Brief

## Working title
**How Picorules Uses AI in 2026**

## Core message
A common assumption is that clinical AI means sending all data to a large language model and asking it for an answer.

That is **not** the TKC approach.

TKC uses **frontier AI models upstream** to accelerate research, synthesis, and rule authoring, then converts that knowledge into **Picorules**: deterministic, testable clinical algorithms that can be validated and deployed safely in production.

## One-sentence version
**AI helps design the logic; Picorules delivers the production decision.**

## Suggested infographic narrative

### 1. Naive AI assumption
**“Put the patient data into the LLM and get the answer back.”**

- Fast to imagine
- Hard to validate
- Hard to audit
- Not ideal for production-grade clinical decision support

### 2. TKC / Picorules approach
**Use frontier models to help build the rule, not to replace the rule.**

AI is used to:

- research guidelines and evidence
- compare existing ruleblocks and clinical patterns
- draft Picorules logic
- refine thresholds, structure, and documentation

Then the output is turned into:

- **deterministic Picorules algorithms**
- compiled and reviewed logic
- testable and auditable outputs
- production-ready clinical decision support

### 3. Production pipeline
**Research -> Rule design -> Deterministic logic -> Testing -> Validation -> Production**

Short caption:

> Frontier models accelerate knowledge work. Picorules turns that work into reliable clinical logic.
